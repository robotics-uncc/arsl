## Lab Director

<div class="person" markdown="1">
<div class="person-photo"><img src="{{ "/assets/images/2023-04/Wolek_sq.png" | relative_url }}" alt="Portrait of Artur Wolek"></div>
<div class="person-body" markdown="1">

Artur Wolek is an Assistant Professor in the [Department of Mechanical Engineering and Engineering Science](https://mees.charlotte.edu/) at The University of North Carolina at Charlotte, a resident faculty member of the NC Battery Complexity, Autonomous Vehicle and Electrification Research Center [(BATT CAVE](https://battcave.charlotte.edu/)) and an affiliate faculty member of the Charlotte Aviation Innovation and Research Institute ([Charlotte AIR](https://air.charlotte.edu/)). Wolek earned the B.S. and Ph.D. degrees in Aerospace Engineering from Virginia Tech in 2010 and 2015. Previously, he was a Postdoctoral Fellow with the Acoustics Division at the U.S. Naval Research Laboratory (2015-2018), an Adjunct Lecturer in the Electrical Engineering and Computer Science Department at Catholic University of America (2017-2018), and a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Institute for Systems Research and Department of Aerospace Engineering at the University of Maryland (2018-2020). Wolek joined UNC Charlotte in 2020 and founded the Autonomous Robots and Systems Laboratory (ARSL). In 2023, Wolek was part of the AFOSR Summer Faculty Fellowship Program collaborating with the Control Science Center at Wright Patterson AFB. In 2026, he was awarded the Undergraduate Teaching Excellence Award by the William States Lee College of Engineering at UNC Charlotte. Wolek has authored more than 55 journal articles, book chapters, and conference proceedings. His research interests are in the area of aerospace and ocean vehicle guidance, dynamics, control and path planning. Wolek teaches undergraduate and graduate-level courses in engineering dynamics, robotics, estimation, aerospace engineering, flight mechanics, and astromechanics. Wolek’s research has been supported by the NSF, AFOSR, Air Force Research Lab, the North Carolina Department of Transportation, the Army STTR Program, Defensewerx, and the Coastal Studies Institute. Wolek has also collaborated with the USAF Test Pilot School and the Naval Research Laboratory. At UNC Charlotte, Wolek is the Faculty Advisor for the AIAA Student Branch, the Design-Build-Fly (DBF) Club, and the Charlotte Space Program. Wolek is a Senior Member of AIAA, a Senior Member of IEEE, an affiliate member of IFAC, and a member of AUVSI and ASME. Wolek serves as an Associate Editor for the ASME Journal of Autonomous Vehicles and Systems (JAVS) and Frontiers in Robotics and AI.
